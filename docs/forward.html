Where the guesses came from

Before this test, the news log (Aug 5 – Oct 30, 2026, 62 trading days) was checked against the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100, gold and long Treasuries: the day of the news, the opening gap and the next 1–5 days. Of 102 checks, 8 passed where about 5 would pass by luck. The strongest (upbeat news days followed by lower bonds and gold the next day) went the other way over the 20 years before, and a striking one in the major-events list since 2000 (bonds giving back their jump the day after a news-driven sell-off) disappeared when the events were picked by something visible on the day rather than with hindsight. News written after the fact can't be tested by looking back, so the most promising ideas were fixed as these ten guesses and are now scored only on what comes next.
