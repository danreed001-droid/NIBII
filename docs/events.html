How this list works

New events are added each night when SPY moves 2.5% or more in a day (with the day's headline), the Fed changes rates, the VIX closes above 30 for the first time in three months, or the market enters a correction, a bear market or a new bull run; those carry an auto tag. A big day's headline comes from the daily news log when it has one for that day, else from MarketWatch or Google News (named in brackets); Fed rate changes are read from the Fed's own FOMC statements. The 1-month figure fills in a month later. Search matches the event text, its date or year, and its tag; the move filter uses the Day or 1 month column, and events without that figure yet drop out while it's on.
